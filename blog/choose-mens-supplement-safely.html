How to Choose a Men's Vitality Supplement Safely

A practical checklist to avoid the risky corners of the men’s supplement market.

By the Spartamax Editorial Team · Updated November 23, 2026

Men’s vitality supplements are a crowded market, and not every product is what it claims to be. The FDA keeps a public list of sexual enhancement and energy products found to contain hidden drug ingredients. Here is how to shop more safely.

1. Understand How Supplements Are Regulated

The FDA does not approve dietary supplements before they are sold. Manufacturers are responsible for safety and for truthful labels. Be wary of any product that claims “FDA approval.”

2. Read the Ingredient List

Look for named ingredients, not vague “proprietary” claims, and compare amounts on the Supplement Facts panel.

3. Check Manufacturing Claims

GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) certification and third-party testing are useful signals. Keep in mind that FDA facility registration is not product approval.

4. Be Skeptical of Instant Promises

Products promising dramatic results within minutes or hours are the ones most often flagged in FDA warnings.

5. Know the Refund Terms

Read the guarantee before you buy: how many days you have, whether opened bottles qualify, and who pays return shipping.

6. Talk to Your Doctor

This matters most if you take nitrates, blood-pressure medication or blood thinners, or if you have heart, liver or hormone-sensitive conditions.
