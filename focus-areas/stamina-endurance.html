Built for Men Who Want to Keep Going

Stamina means different things to different men: a longer run, a full day of physical work, or simply not fading by mid-afternoon. Spartamax includes three ingredients with this goal in mind.

Dietary Nitrate and Performance

Beet root is one of the richest natural sources of dietary nitrate. A 2024 meta-analysis of 27 studies in healthy men reported modest gains in repetitions to failure and in time to exhaustion with beetroot-based supplements. Those studies used concentrated doses, and the amount in a Spartamax gummy isn’t published, so treat this as background, not a promise.

Supporting Botanicals

Grape seed is a source of antioxidant proanthocyanidins, which the seller links to blood-vessel health and endurance. Horny goat weed (Epimedium) has been used as a tonic in traditional Chinese practice. MedlinePlus notes the evidence is limited and lists precautions.
