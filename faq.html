What is Spartamax?

Spartamax is a once-daily gummy dietary supplement made for adult men. Each bottle contains 30 gummies, which the seller sells as a 30-day supply. You can read the full breakdown on the What Is It page.

The seller lists seven ingredients: L-arginine HCl, tongkat ali, maca root, ashwagandha, horny goat weed, beet root and grape seed. The seller’s directions are to take one gummy daily, as directed on the product label, as part of a consistent routine. Don’t take more than the label says. The How It Works page has simple tips for building the habit.
